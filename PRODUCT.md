# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Local individuals and families in and around Pudukkottai and Peravurani seeking healthy weight loss, weight gain, personalized nutrition plans, and sustainable daily habits without extreme diets or strenuous workouts.

## Product Purpose
Pudugai Wellness Center (PWC) guides people toward lasting, vibrant health through simple daily habits, 1-on-1 personal coaching, and non-invasive body composition assessments. Success means clients feel energized, achieve healthy body composition milestones, and build sustainable routines they can maintain for life.

## Positioning
Accessible, friendly community wellness: dual local physical centers (Pudukkottai & Peravurani) backed by direct personal coaching from Coach Thangapandian S and Coach Thenmozhi, 5-point body composition screening, and custom meal plans tailored to everyday local foods—without strict diets or clinical intimidation.

## Operating Context
- Physical visits to Pudukkottai or Peravurani wellness centers for body composition checks and consultations.
- Direct mobile communication via WhatsApp and phone calls for ongoing daily habit support and plan adjustments.
- Lightweight administrative control for coaches to track enquiries, update client stories, and manage programs.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Public informational website: multi-location visibility, programs & checks breakdown, coaches story, client testimonials, and WhatsApp enquiry intake.
- Administrative management portal: secure coach login, hero carousel management, published services, success stories, and customer enquiry tracking.
- Strict non-medical boundary: lifestyle and educational wellness only; does not provide clinical diagnoses, medical treatments, or guaranteed rapid weight-loss claims.
- Shared single helpline (`94434 43261`) serving both centers.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Pudugai Wellness Center (PWC).
- Key Figures: Coach Thangapandian S (Pudukkottai) & Coach Thenmozhi (Peravurani).
- Visual Identity: Dark forest green brand palette (`#134e32`), warm off-white surfaces (`#f8faf8`), and clean minimalist typography with Tamil language accessibility.
- Tone: Warm, welcoming, respectful, encouraging, and clear everyday English and Tamil.

## Evidence on Hand
- Two verified physical branch locations with Google Maps listings.
- Real client testimonials and transformation case studies with duration milestones and bilingual support.
- Verified 5-point screening protocols (BMI, BMR, Body Fat, Metabolic Age, Skeletal Muscle).
- Authentic founder journey from part-time passion in Dec 2019 to established full-time community wellness centers.

## Product Principles
1. **Simplicity Over Rigidity:** Sustainable daily habits and familiar foods outperform extreme diets and short-term fads.
2. **Community Trust First:** Transparent, genuine guidance with clear non-medical disclaimers; no exaggerated claims or fabricated results.
3. **Dual-Branch Inclusivity:** Both Pudukkottai and Peravurani centers are equal pillars of the community wellness mission.
4. **Frictionless Direct Contact:** Clear, instant WhatsApp and phone access for consultations without complex onboarding barriers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Mobile-first responsive experience accommodating standard, compact, and budget smartphones (320px–430px).
- High-contrast text on light surfaces adhering to WCAG 2.1 AA readability standards.
- Bilingual cultural awareness catering to English and Tamil speaking community members.
